How it will work

Three steps, built so donors can trust every dollar and people asking for help keep their dignity.

1

Upload and verify

A person uploads one real bill. We verify it, check it is not already paid or posted twice, and redact private details like account numbers before anyone sees it.

2

Pick a bill, or go random

Donors browse by category, amount, or city, or tap Random Bill and get matched with one verified bill under a cap they set. No endless scrolling required.

3

Paid direct, proof posted

The money goes straight to the biller, never as cash to the requester. When the biller confirms payment, the bill closes and the donor's profile updates.

Built on trust, on purpose

Fake giveaways are everywhere online. This only works if it is provably different.

Never cash

Donations pay the biller directly. Money never passes through the hands of the person asking, which stops most abuse before it starts.

Verified bills only

Every bill is checked against the biller, screened for duplicates, and stripped of private details before it is shown to donors.

Not just billionaires

Billionaires are welcome, and so are creators, local businesses, churches, and anyone who can clear one $87 electric bill and change someone's week.

Questions

Straight answers, including the parts still being decided.

When does it launch?

There is no date yet. This waitlist is how we measure real interest before building the full app. Waitlist members, on both sides, get invited first.

Do I have to be a billionaire to pay a bill?

No. Billionaires can clear a lot of bills, but the heart of this is anyone with a little extra clearing one real bill for one real person, and getting credit for it if they want it.

What bills will qualify?

The launch plan starts with utilities, rent, phone, and medical bills, because they are the easiest to verify and pay directly. Credit cards and loans are held for later.

Is paying someone's bill tax deductible?

Paying an individual's bill directly is generally a gift, not a deductible charitable donation. The final structure is still being decided, so do not count on a deduction. We will state it plainly before anyone pays a cent.

Should I send you my bill now?

No. Do not send bills, account numbers, or documents at this stage. Join the waitlist and we will tell you exactly when and how verified uploads open.
