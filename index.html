The honest answers

A few things you might be wondering.

When will it launch?

There isn’t a launch date yet. We’re using this waitlist to see if people want the idea before building the full service. Joining means we can send you updates and early access details if we move forward.

Can I upload a bill or donate now?

No. This is an early concept page. Please don’t send bills, account numbers, payment details, or money. We’ll explain the process if bill submissions or payments open in the future.

How would a bill get paid?

The idea is to verify a bill, protect private details, and send any payment directly to the biller. Those steps still need to be built and tested before anyone can use the service.

Would a payment be tax deductible?

We haven’t established a final legal or tax structure. Don’t assume payments would be tax deductible. We’ll make the terms clear before accepting any payment.

Do I have to give a lot to help?

No. The idea is built around people helping at the level that works for them—even one smaller bill can mean a lot to the person behind it.
